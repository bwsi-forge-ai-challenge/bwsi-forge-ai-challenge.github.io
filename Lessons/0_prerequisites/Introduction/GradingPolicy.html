Grading Policy

This page summarizes how your final grade is determined and the course policies on completing work and deadlines.

1. Grade breakdown

Your final grade is weighted as follows:

ComponentWeight
Unit quizzes10%
Coding assignments15%
Project 120%
Project 220%
Project 335%

2. Completion requirement

Important

You can only pass the class if you complete every assignment. Missing an assignment is not compatible with passing, regardless of your performance on the rest of the work.

3. Deadlines, late work, and extensions

Gradescope accepts late submissions during a 7-day grace period after each deadline. Work submitted during the grace period loses 10% of the available credit for each day it is late. For example, an assignment submitted two days late that would have earned 90% receives 70%.

After day 7, the grace period ends and the assignment is worth 0%. Because every assignment must be completed to pass the class, make sure to submit within the grace period even if you are running late.

If you foresee any reason why you cannot make a deadline, please let us know in advance. It is much easier for us to accommodate you when we have ample heads-up.
