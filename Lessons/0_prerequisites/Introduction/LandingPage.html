Course Introduction

Welcome to the F.O.R.G.E. AI Challenge! This 6-month program is designed to take you from foundational mathematical concepts to building, evaluating, and deploying advanced, safe, and multimodal generative AI systems.

No prior experience with machine learning is required. Throughout the program, our targeted curriculum will help you develop the mathematical foundations and modeling techniques needed to understand and work with state-of-the-art generative AI systems.

Our Core Goals

  • Introduce the foundational mathematics of Machine Learning and Generative AI.
  • Understand the inner workings of transformer models and their real-world applications.
  • Gain hands-on experience processing multimodal data (text, images, and audio).
  • Learn principled prompting and discover how to build ethically responsible, safe AI systems.

Course Roadmap

Timeline Focus Area Milestone / Major Project
Month 0 Prerequisites: Linear Algebra, Calculus, Probability, and Intro to ML Build a Predictive Model
Month 1 Foundations of Generative AI (Tokens, Embeddings, Transformers) Build a Transformer from Scratch
Month 2 Multimodal & Advanced Models (Image Generation, Diffusion, Audio AI) Project 1: Multimodal/Interactive Game Demo
Months 3-4 Building Systems (RAG, Vector Databases, APIs, Agents, RLHF) Project 2: End-to-End RAG System & Adversarial Testing
Month 5 Prompting & Advanced LLM Usage (Structured Outputs, Cost Optimization) Final Project Release
Month 6 Safety, Evaluation Frameworks, and Adversarial Thinking Final Project: Multimodal Puzzle-Solving Agent Competition

What Lies Ahead: The Final Challenge

During Month 6, you will put everything you have learned to the test by participating in a real ML conference-style competition. You will design a Multimodal Puzzle-Solving Agent capable of tackling logic, audio, text, and image problems, competing for the top spot on our leaderboard!

Course Prerequisites

To ensure your success in this course, all students must have already completed the BWSI CSS Course. We also expect Python fluency as well, as we'll be using Python-based machine learning frameeworks in this course.
