Course Communication

This page covers how we communicate during the course and how you submit coding assignments. Check here (and Piazza) whenever you need course logistics.

1. Piazza

The course uses Piazza for announcements, updates, and discussion. Instructors will post important information there, including schedule changes, assignment clarifications, and other course news. You should check Piazza regularly so you do not miss updates. We'll be using Piazza to announce the release and deadline of any assignments and projects throughout this course.

You may discuss course material with classmates on Piazza. Asking questions, sharing resources, and helping one another understand concepts is encouraged. Do not post solutions, completed code, or other answers to graded work. If you are unsure whether something would give away an answer, do not post it.

Be respectful in all posts and comments. Keep discussion professional, assume good intent, and avoid personal attacks or dismissive language. Piazza is a shared classroom space.

2. Gradescope

Coding assignments are submitted on Gradescope. After you finish a notebook (for example in Colab), download the .ipynb file and upload it to the corresponding Gradescope assignment. Follow any file-name or format instructions listed on that assignment page. The Gradescope will be our one source of truth for all deadlines for all assignments and projects in this course.

Gradescope is for submission and grading, not for general discussion. Use Piazza if you have questions about an assignment before you submit.
