Assignment: Download, Run, and Fine-Tune a Real Model

Apply what you learned in this unit by loading a small open-source model, fine-tuning it on a narrow task, and tracking your training runs with Weights & Biases.

Starter notebook: fine_tune_assignment.ipynb
Environment: Google Colab recommended (free GPU), or local Python with PyTorch.

Download the starter notebook below, complete the TODOs inside it, and run all cells before submitting. The notebook explains every step. You will also need a free Weights & Biases account for Part 3.

Download fine_tune_assignment.ipynb

Submitting your work

  1. Complete every TODO, including both reflections (part1_reflection and part4_reflection), and make sure you have at least 50 training pairs.
  2. Run every cell in the notebook top to bottom and confirm there are no errors.
  3. Export the notebook as a Python script (File → Download → Download .py in Colab) and rename it to firstname_lastname_fine_tune.py.
  4. Submit only that .py file on Gradescope: Fine-Tuning assignment.
