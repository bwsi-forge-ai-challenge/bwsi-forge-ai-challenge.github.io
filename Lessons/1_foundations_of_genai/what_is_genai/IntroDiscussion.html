Discussion: Introduce Yourself

Welcome to Foundations of Generative AI! Before starting the first lesson, your first assignment is to introduce yourself to the class on Piazza.

  1. On Piazza, find the Introduction to GenAI: Introduce Yourself discussion post.
  2. Reply to the post with a short response that covers the three points below.
  1. Introduce yourself. Share your name, where you are from, and anything else you would like your classmates to know about you.
  2. What are you excited to learn? Name a topic, tool, or skill from this course that you are most looking forward to.
  3. What is generative AI? Before looking at the course content, describe in your own words your current understanding of what generative AI is. There is no wrong answer here.

Take a moment to read and reply to a few of your classmates' introductions as well.
