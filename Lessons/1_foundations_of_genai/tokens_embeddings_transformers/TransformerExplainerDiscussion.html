Discussion: Explore a Real Transformer

You have now seen every piece of a transformer. In this discussion you will open a real one, GPT-2 small, and share what you find with the class on Piazza.

Transformer Explainer runs GPT-2 small, the model the last lesson used for every number, inside your browser. You can type a prompt and click into every box of the pipeline to see the actual numbers.

Before you start

  • Use a laptop or desktop. On a phone you can only try the built-in examples.
  • The first visit downloads the model, which can take a minute.
  • Prompts are limited to 12 words. Click Generate to predict the next token.
  1. On Piazza, find the Tokens, Embeddings & Transformers: Explore a Real Transformer discussion post.
  2. Spend some time exploring the tool. Try your own prompts, click into the boxes, and change the settings to see what happens.
  3. Reply to the post with what you found, covering the three points below. Include your prompts so classmates can try them too.
  4. Reply to at least two classmates.

Your post

  1. Something you noticed. Pick one part of the pipeline you clicked into, such as the tokens, the embeddings, the attention, or the probabilities at the end. What did you see there, and did it match what you expected from the lessons?
  2. An experiment. Find a prompt that makes GPT-2 do something interesting: a surprisingly good guess, a strange or wrong one, or a big change when you edit a single word or slide a setting. Share the prompt and what happened, and give your best guess at why.
  3. A question. Share one thing you are still curious or confused about after exploring. It can be about the tool, the model, or how something from the lessons shows up in it.
