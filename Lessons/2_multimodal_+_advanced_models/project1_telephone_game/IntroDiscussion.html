Discussion: Pitch Your Style

Before starting Project 1, pitch the style you are thinking of to the class on Piazza and get feedback.

  1. On Piazza, find the Project 1: Pitch Your Style discussion post.
  2. Reply to the post with a short response that covers the three points below.
  1. Your style. Describe your style persona in two sentences, and how it would show up in a caption, a piece of music, and an image.
  2. Why is it hard for a base model? Why do you think the base models cannot already produce this style from a prompt alone?
  3. Feedback for others. Reply to two classmates. Is their style recognizable in all three, and what would you change?

Remember the project rules on styles: no living artist's or band's signature style, and no copyrighted songs or characters.
