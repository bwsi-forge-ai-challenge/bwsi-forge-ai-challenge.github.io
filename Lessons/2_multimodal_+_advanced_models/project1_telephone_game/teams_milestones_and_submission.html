Project 1: Teams, Milestones, and Submission

How to find a partner, what is due when, and how to submit as a team on Gradescope. Read the project overview first.

1. Forming a Team

Project 1 is done in pairs. Working in a pair mirrors how real ML projects are built: you will split the stations between you, then combine them into one pipeline.

  1. Find a partner. Reply to the pinned Project 1: Search for Teammates post on Piazza with your time zone and when you are usually available. Pick someone whose schedule overlaps with yours.
  2. Register together by submitting Milestone 0 as a group on Gradescope (see below). A team exists once Milestone 0 lists both names.
  3. No partner by the Milestone 0 deadline? Submit Milestone 0 on your own, and staff will pair you with another unmatched student within two days. If the class has an odd number of students, one team of three is formed; that team completes three extensions instead of two.

The 65 points for the final submission cover the game loop and measurement (20), extensions (20), and report (25). See the grading table in the project overview.

Late work

  • Milestone 1 and the final submission follow the course late policy: 10% off per day, up to the 7-day grace period.
  • Milestone 0 cannot be late, because staff form teams for unmatched students right after the deadline.
  • Game Day cannot be made up, because it is a live class event. If you have an excused absence, tell staff in advance; you will run your stations on the Game Day files afterwards and submit a short write-up instead.

3. Submitting as a Team on Gradescope

Milestones 0, 1, and 2 are group submissions: one partner submits for the whole team, and both partners receive the grade.

  1. One partner uploads the files to the Gradescope assignment.
  2. In the upload window, choose Add Group Member and select your partner. If you forget, open the submission afterwards and add your partner from the group members option at the bottom of the page.
  3. Your partner should check that the submission appears in their own Gradescope account.
  4. To change your submission, either partner can resubmit before the deadline. Only the latest submission is graded.

The peer evaluation is individual: each student submits their own, and it is kept private from your partner.

4. Templates

Milestone 0: Team plan (one page)

  1. Team members and the best way to reach each of you.
  2. Who owns which stations and which shared parts.
  3. Extensions: the two you plan to do (three for a team of three), and which partner leads each. For "Your own idea", describe it in 3–4 sentences.
  4. Models you plan to start with, if different from the starter models.
  5. Starting images: your ideas for at least 5 varied images, and where they will come from.
  6. Schedule: when you will meet each week.

Final report (3–5 pages, PDF)

  1. Setup: the model used for each station and any settings you chose, such as caption length or random seeds.
  2. Results: your drift plots for all starting images and a summary table of the metrics by round.
  3. Analysis: which station loses the most meaning and why, supported by at least two example chains shown step by step. Connect what you see to the module lessons: compression, invention, and drift toward typical content.
  4. Extensions: for each, the question, what you changed, the measured comparison with your baseline, and what you conclude.
  5. Limitations: what your metrics miss, and what you would test next.
  6. Contribution statement: what each partner did.
  7. AI-use statement: how you used AI tools, as required by the course policy.
  8. Image credits: the source of every starting image you did not create.

5. If Something Goes Wrong

  • Your partner is unresponsive or not contributing: message course staff privately on Piazza as early as possible, and no later than the Milestone 1 deadline. Staff can only help with problems they know about.
  • Colab GPU limits: free GPU time is limited each day. Test on one image first, save chains to Google Drive as you go, and avoid re-running models you do not need. If you are blocked, post on Piazza.
  • A model will not load or run: check the model card on Hugging Face for example code, then ask on Piazza. Include the full error message.
