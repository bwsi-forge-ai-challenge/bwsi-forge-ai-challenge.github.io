Discussion: Describing a Sound

Before starting the audio deep dive, share your thinking with the class on Piazza.

  1. On Piazza, find the Audio Generation: Describing a Sound discussion post.
  2. Reply to the post with a short response that covers the three points below.
  1. Put a sound into words. Pick a sound you hear every day (a kettle, rain, a bus, a friend laughing) and describe it in words so that someone else could imagine it. What was easy to say, and what could you not put into words?
  2. Your own prompt. If you could ask an AI to generate any sound or piece of music, what would you ask for, and how would you phrase the prompt?
  3. Sound inside a computer. Before looking at the lessons, how do you think a computer stores a sound? There is no wrong answer here.

Take a moment to read and reply to a few of your classmates' posts as well.
