Assignment: From Sound to Tokens

Follow a melody from note numbers to a waveform, then to a spectrogram, then to a short stream of tokens from a tiny sound dictionary: a hands-on version of the pipeline from this unit.

Starter notebook: audio_tokens_assignment.ipynb
Environment: Google Colab or local Python with NumPy and Matplotlib. A CPU is enough; no GPU or downloads are needed.
Suggested time: about 1 hour
Companion reading: Turning Sound into Numbers and The Sound Dictionary

Download the starter notebook below, upload it to Google Colab (File → Upload notebook), complete the TODOs inside it, and run all cells before submitting. Each part has a short explanation and a written reflection.

Download audio_tokens_assignment.ipynb

What You Will Do

PartTaskWhat it shows
1Convert MIDI note numbers to frequencies, play a melody, and record a high note at two sample ratesWhy the sample rate must be more than twice the highest frequency
2Cut the waveform into overlapping frames and view the spectrogram with a short and a long windowThe trade-off between seeing when and seeing which pitch
3Snap spectrogram frames to their nearest codebook entry, then stack four codebooksHow residual codebooks turn audio into a short stream of tokens, coarse to fine

Each part ends with a short written reflection, stored as a Python string in the notebook.

Submitting Your Work

  1. Complete every TODO and all three reflections (part1_reflection, part2_reflection, and part3_reflection).
  2. Run every cell in the notebook from top to bottom without errors.
  3. Export the notebook as a Python script (File → Download → Download .py in Colab) and rename it to firstname_lastname_audio.py.
  4. Submit only that .py file on Gradescope: From Sound to Tokens assignment.
