Discussion: Break a Vision-Language Model

Before starting this deep dive, try to fool a vision-language model and share what happened with the class on Piazza.

  1. On Piazza, find the Vision-Language Models: Break a Model discussion post.
  2. Reply to the post with a short response that covers the three points below.
  1. Ask it a hard question. Upload a photo to a free vision-language model demo (for example, one of the vision-language Spaces on Hugging Face) and ask a question that needs counting, reading small text, or judging where things are. What did you ask, and what did it answer?
  2. Why do you think so? Did it get the answer right? If not, what do you guess went wrong? There is no wrong answer here; you will learn the real causes in this unit.
  3. Compare mistakes. Reply to a classmate who found a different kind of mistake, and say whether you think it has the same cause as yours.

Take a moment to read a few of your classmates' posts as well.
