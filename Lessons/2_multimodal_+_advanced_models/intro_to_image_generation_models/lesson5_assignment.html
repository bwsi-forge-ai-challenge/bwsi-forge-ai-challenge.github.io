Assignment: Noise to Picture

Destroy a picture with noise, train a tiny diffusion model of your own, turn pure noise into new pictures, morph between them, and then try a big pretrained model with your own prompts.

Starter notebook: diffusion_assignment.ipynb
Environment: Google Colab with a free T4 GPU. The notebook downloads everything else it needs (a small picture dataset and the SD-Turbo model, about 2.5 GB).
Suggested time: 2 to 2.5 hours
Companion reading: Generating Images by Removing Noise: Diffusion

Download the starter notebook below, upload it to Google Colab (File → Upload notebook), complete the TODOs inside it, and run all cells before submitting. Each part has a short explanation, a check cell that tells you whether your code is right, and a written reflection.

Download diffusion_assignment.ipynb

What You Will Do

PartTaskWhat it shows
1Write make_alpha_bar and add_noise, then watch a picture disappear into noiseHow the noise schedule destroys a picture
2Write predict_clean and recover the picture from a noise guess, a perfect one and a slightly wrong oneWhy a perfect noise prediction matters, and why generating takes many small steps
3Write noise_prediction_loss and train a tiny U-Net on small pictures (about 3 to 5 minutes on a T4)What a diffusion model actually learns
4Write the ddim_sample loop and generate new pictures with different numbers of stepsHow noise becomes a picture, and what extra steps cost
5Write slerp and morph one generated picture into anotherWhy the starting noise is the "seed" of a picture
6Write four prompts that grow more detailed, run them on SD-Turbo, and pick a favoriteHow text prompts steer a large model
7Reflect on responsible use of image generatorsCapability is not permission

Every part ends with a short written reflection, stored as a Python string in the notebook. The reflections are a large part of the grade.

Submitting Your Work

  1. Complete every TODO and all seven reflections (part1_reflection to part7_reflection). Keep every function name and signature exactly as given: the autograder tests your functions on its own small models and inputs.
  2. Run every cell in the notebook from top to bottom and confirm that every check cell passes.
  3. Export the notebook as a Python script (File → Download → Download .py in Colab) and rename it to firstname_lastname_diffusion.py.
  4. Submit only that .py file on Gradescope: Noise to Picture assignment.

If you can't access the Gradescope link, go to the Gradescope for this class (if you are logged in you should be able to see this course when you visit the Gradescope home page), select this course, and then pick the Noise to Picture assignment.
