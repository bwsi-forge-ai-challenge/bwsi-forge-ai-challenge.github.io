Assignment: Noise to Picture

Destroy a picture with noise, train a tiny diffusion model of your own, and write the loop that turns pure noise into new pictures.

Starter notebook: diffusion_assignment.ipynb
Environment: Google Colab with a free T4 GPU. The notebook downloads the small picture dataset it needs.
Suggested time: about 1 hour
Companion reading: Generating Images by Removing Noise: Diffusion

Download the starter notebook below, upload it to Google Colab (File → Upload notebook), complete the TODOs inside it, and run all cells before submitting. Each part has a short explanation and a written reflection.

Download diffusion_assignment.ipynb

What You Will Do

PartTaskWhat it shows
1Write add_noise, then watch a picture disappear into noiseHow the noise schedule destroys a picture
2Write noise_prediction_loss and train a tiny U-Net on small pictures (about 1 minute on a T4)What a diffusion model actually learns
3Write ddim_step, one noise-removal step, and generate new pictures with different numbers of stepsHow noise becomes a picture, and what extra steps cost

Every part ends with a short written reflection, stored as a Python string in the notebook. The reflections are a large part of the grade.

Submitting Your Work

  1. Complete every TODO and all three reflections (part1_reflection to part3_reflection). Keep every function name and signature exactly as given: the autograder tests your functions on its own small models and inputs.
  2. Run every cell in the notebook from top to bottom without errors.
  3. Export the notebook as a Python script (File → Download → Download .py in Colab) and rename it to firstname_lastname_diffusion.py.
  4. Submit only that .py file on Gradescope: Noise to Picture assignment.

If you can't access the Gradescope link, go to the Gradescope for this class (if you are logged in you should be able to see this course when you visit the Gradescope home page), select this course, and then pick the Noise to Picture assignment.
