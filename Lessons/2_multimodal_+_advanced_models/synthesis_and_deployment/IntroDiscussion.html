Discussion: Who Is Responsible When It's Wrong?

Before starting the last unit, think about what happens when a multimodal system makes a mistake, and share your view with the class on Piazza.

  1. On Piazza, find the Synthesis & Deployment: Who Is Responsible? discussion post.
  2. Reply to the post with a short response that covers the three points below.
  1. A real product. Choose a real product that uses images, sound, or video, such as a medical-imaging assistant, a voice assistant, or a car's driver-assistance camera. Which failure worries you most: making up things that are not there, following hidden instructions placed in an image, or ignoring the image and guessing?
  2. A test you would run. What is one test you would run before trusting it?
  3. Deploy it or not? Would you deploy it? Under what conditions, and who should check its answers? There is no single right answer here.

Take a moment to read and reply to a few of your classmates' posts as well.
