Discussion: When Is Text Not Enough?

Welcome to Core Multimodal Foundations! Before starting the first lesson, share your thinking with the class on Piazza.

  1. On Piazza, find the Core Multimodal Foundations: When Is Text Not Enough? discussion post.
  2. Reply to the post with a short response that covers the three points below.
  1. A task where text falls short. Describe one everyday situation where a text-only AI would give a poor answer because the key information is in a picture, a sound, or a tone of voice.
  2. The hardest kind of input. Which kind of input (images, sound, video, or something else, like touch or motion) do you think is hardest for an AI to handle, and why?
  3. Pictures and words together. Before looking at the course content, describe in your own words how you think an AI could use a photo and a sentence together. There is no wrong answer here.

Take a moment to read and reply to a few of your classmates' posts as well.
