Discussion: Uses and Ethics of Multimodal AI

Welcome to Multimodal & Advanced Models! Before starting the first lesson, share your thinking with the class on Piazza.

  1. On Piazza, find the Multimodal & Advanced Models: Uses and Ethics of Multimodal AI discussion post.
  2. Reply to the post with a short response that covers the two points below.
  3. Reply to at least two classmates.

Your post

  1. What can you use multimodal AI for? Multimodal models work with images, sound, and video as well as text. Describe one or two things you could use them for, in your own life, in a job, or in a project you would like to build.
  2. Ethical concerns and where you draw the line. Music and image generation raise real concerns, such as whose work the models learned from, who gets credit and pay, and how easy it is to fake something. Pick one concern that matters most to you. Then say when you think it is okay to use these tools and when it is not, and explain why.

There are no wrong answers here, and thoughtful people disagree on the second question. Read your classmates' posts too, and feel free to respectfully push back.
